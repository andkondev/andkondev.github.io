Lava Lamp

Slow, looping lava behind your tabs, toolbar and sidebar.

It comes as a theme or an extension: both are for Firefox on a computer, keep text readable over the lava and collect nothing.

Theme

  • The original
  • One look
  • No settings
  • Under 7 MB
Get the theme

Extension

  • Adds the panel shown here
  • Three looks, any color
  • Speed, size, amount and a sidebar strip
  • About 55 MB, Firefox 140 or later

Size, Amount and sidebar changes take a few seconds to build, once. Bigger settings use more memory.

Get the extension

The lava could not start in this browser.

Privacy

Neither the extension nor the theme collects anything.

  • The extension keeps your settings, and any animation it has made, in Firefox on your computer. It asks for two permissions: theme, to show the lava, and storage, to remember your settings. It makes no network requests and has no analytics, accounts or ads.
  • The theme is colors and one animated image. It contains no code.
  • The demo on this page runs in your browser. What you choose here is not saved or sent anywhere.
